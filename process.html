The Five Phases

Every step, explained.

No black boxes. Here's exactly what we do at each phase of your engagement and why it matters.

01
Phase One

Discovery

We start by going deep. Before we recommend a single thing, we audit your current digital presence, analyze your competitors, map out your target audience, and understand the business objectives behind your marketing goals. This phase ensures every decision we make is grounded in reality — not assumptions.

Technical SEO audit Competitor gap analysis Keyword landscape report Goals alignment session
02
Phase Two

Strategy

Discovery without a plan is just research. We translate what we've learned into a custom roadmap tailored to your business. This includes prioritized action items, clear KPIs, a content plan, technical fixes, and a timeline with milestones. You'll know exactly what we're doing and why.

90-day roadmap KPI framework Content calendar Priority action list
03
Phase Three

Execution

This is where the work happens. Our team executes on the strategy — building content, fixing technical issues, building authority, optimizing pages, and implementing everything needed to move the needle. We work fast without cutting corners, and you're kept in the loop throughout.

On-page optimizations Content production Link building Technical fixes
04
Phase Four

Optimize

Execution gets you results. Optimization compounds them. We continuously monitor what's working and what isn't, then refine. A/B testing, content updates, technical improvements, and ranking adjustments are all part of keeping your performance on an upward trajectory.

Rank tracking & analysis Conversion optimization Content refreshes Algorithm monitoring
05
Phase Five

Report & Scale

Every month you receive a clear, branded performance report tied directly to the KPIs we defined together. No vanity metrics. We walk you through what moved, why it moved, and what we're doing next. Then we look at how to scale what's working into the next phase of growth.

Monthly performance report Quarterly strategy review Growth recommendations Scaling roadmap
